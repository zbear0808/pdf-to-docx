# Instructions

When asked to critically check a draft (worksheet, test, quiz, lesson plan, or problem set), follow these steps:

1. **Review for Verbosity:** Read through the document to identify any sentences or phrases that are overly verbose, dramatic, conversational, or unnecessarily wordy. Pay special attention to bloated question stems and teacher directions.
2. **Remove Redundancy:** Identify and cut out sections, sentences, or explanatory clauses that are redundant or do not contribute meaningful instructional data or problem constraints.
3. **Punctuation & Character Cleanup:** Eliminate rarely used characters such as em dashes ("—" or "--"). Replace them with clean, natural punctuation (such as commas, parentheses, or splitting into separate sentences).
4. **Preserve Structure & Rigor:** Your primary goal is to cut out useless material and fluff. Do NOT fundamentally change the problem numbering, question options (A-D), mathematical numbers/formulas, code segments, or layout structures. Ensure the tone remains direct, clear, and straightforward.
5. **Direct Edits:** Apply the edits directly to the draft while leaving the core content, problem integrity, and document organization intact.
