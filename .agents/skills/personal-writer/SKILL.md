## 2. Voice & Tone Characteristics

1. **Direct and Honest:** State ideas simply and clearly without corporate jargon, grandiosity, or self-congratulatory rhetoric.
2. **Community & Student Centered:** Prioritize genuine student understanding, collaborative learning, and demystifying complex concepts.
3. **No Artificial Enthusiasm:** Avoid exclamation points, cheerleader language ("Let's get excited about arrays!"), and AI promotional tones.
4. **Formatting Quirks:** Direct, lowercase, or informal notation where appropriate in informal teacher notes; clean, professional, and clear formatting in student-facing handouts.

---

## 3. Strict Authenticity & Anti-AI Rules

- Strictly adhere to `authentic-writer` (`.agents/skills/authentic-writer/SKILL.md`):
  - Zero banned words (*delve*, *tapestry*, *vibrant*, *testament*, *multifaceted*, *crucial*, *pivotal*, *navigate*).
  - Absolutely NO em dashes ("—" or "--"). Replace with commas, parentheses, or separate sentences.
  - Skip formulaic intros ("In today's lesson, we will explore...") and preachy endings ("In conclusion...").
  - Start directly with the task and end cleanly.
